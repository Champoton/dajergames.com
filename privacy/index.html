Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Castlewhip ("the game"), made by Dajer Games, collects and how it is used. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress — coins, records, unlocked heroes, whips and skins, settings and the player name you choose — is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted if you delete the game.

2. Leaderboards (Game Center)

If you use Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores are shared with Apple to show leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The player name you type in the game may be shown to other players in the rankings, so please don't use personal information in it.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We only learn which items were purchased so the game can unlock them.

4. Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general location. On iOS, the game will ask your permission before any tracking across apps (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads may be less relevant but will still appear. Learn more in Google's advertising policies. You can remove ads by purchasing No Ads Forever.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts or photos through the game.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
